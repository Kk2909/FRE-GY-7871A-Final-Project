# Frozen direct-AI measurement and audit
Primary input: title alone, with source/date metadata hidden in shuffled inputs. Metadata can still be inferred from the wording. Assistant labels are AI-assisted; there are no human raters. The precise assistant weight snapshot and sampling parameters are unavailable. Frozen outputs, rather than a claim of identical future regeneration, support numerical reproducibility.

Tone -1: explicit adverse economic conditions, hardship, higher household costs, job losses or pessimistic economic forecasts. Tone +1: explicit improvements, relief, lower household costs, job gains or optimistic economic forecasts. Tone0: no directional claim, unanswered questions, generic methods/resources, or mixed improvements and adverse conditions without a clear overall direction. Bare policy announcements/proposals do not automatically imply economic welfare. Factual adversity is negative tone but not evidence of media bias. Claimed political success can be positive tone without certifying the claim.

Geography/relevance: US macroeconomic, household, labor, housing and economic-policy content, including state/local experiences. Exclude clearly foreign-only conditions, cryptocurrency-only narratives without US economic conditions, self-promotion, non-economic metaphor and unrelated human-interest content. Generic titles from US economic publishers use contextual relevance, retained as an uncertainty tier. Relevance review excludes21/691 initially sampled records. No financial result guides inclusion.

Sampling: select sources/months on access/coverage only. NPR, Colorado Sun and Hacker News each require3 eligible titles. Of the initial max20 hash-selected titles per source/month, use the first5 by the frozen sampling hash in47 balanced windows.691 are selected;670 remain in scope, each outlet/month retaining3-5. Equal means within outlets, then equal1/3 source/stratum weights. No interpolation, audience weights, likes or comments.

Review:120 exact-record earlier blind AI-reference labels are reused. A shuffled second pass covers150 titles,50/source.147 agree. Three disagreements and ten uniform policy clarifications are preserved. Same-session carryover/shared errors can inflate agreement. Two independent human labels required by the original brief are not completed in this user-selected AI-assisted alternative.

Other dimensions use frozen literal proxies in score_corpus_v7.py: overlapping prices/labor/housing/growth/debt/policy vocabulary shares, explicit elevated-price-level/rate framing, political mentions and co-occurring blame words, and strong-affect vocabulary. These measure wording in sampled titles, not full semantic coverage, factual accuracy, political persuasion or cognition. Factual claims require matched official release/metric/period/vintage. Matched-release cases remain purposive illustrations, not representative prevalence estimates.

Financial models use no new labels until all primary coding and review decisions are frozen. The failed semantic/hybrid classifiers remain rejected measurement evidence. Neither their moderate development metrics nor98% primary AI consistency is independent human accuracy.
